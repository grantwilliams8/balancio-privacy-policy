Balancio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Balancio ("the App") is a casual balance puzzle game developed by Grant Williams ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used, and your choices.

Summary

Balancio does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, or other contact information. Your gameplay progress (levels completed, stars earned, streaks, preferences) is stored only on your device. The App shows advertising through Google AdMob and supports optional in‑app purchases through Apple's App Store, each of which may collect limited technical data as described below.

Information We Collect Directly

We do not operate any backend servers for Balancio and do not directly collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Gameplay data such as:

  • Level progress and star ratings
  • Daily streaks and best scores
  • Selected cosmetic items (beam, weight, and background visuals)
  • Settings preferences (e.g. sound, haptics)

is stored locally on your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes this information.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Balancio uses the following third-party services, which may independently collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies:

ServicePurposeData
Google AdMob Displays in-app advertising Device identifiers (such as the IDFA, only with your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), approximate location, ad interaction data, and other technical data used to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
Apple App Store (StoreKit) Processes optional in-app purchases Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment information. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the first time the App may use tracking-related identifiers for advertising, you will be shown Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can allow or deny this at any time, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device. If you decline, the App will show non-personalized ads where available instead.

Children's Privacy

Balancio is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because gameplay data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the App. We do not retain any copy of this data. Any data collected by third-party services (AdMob) is retained and deleted according to that provider's own policies.

Your Choices

  • Manage App Tracking Transparency permission in your device Settings.
  • Disable personalized ads via Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising and your device's general ad-tracking controls.
  • Delete all local app data by uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
